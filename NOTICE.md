## Art assets

The sprites, backgrounds and UI art under `assets/` are **not** covered by the MIT
licence. They were collected for a university coursework project and several of them
originate from the commercial game *20 Minutes Till Dawn* by flanne. They are included
here for educational and non-commercial use only.

If you intend to fork this project for anything beyond study, replace the contents of
`assets/` with art you have the right to use. The code does not depend on any specific
image beyond the filenames it loads, so swapping them is mostly a matter of keeping the
same names.
